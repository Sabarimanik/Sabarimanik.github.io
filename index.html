About Me

I am an Embedded Engineer currently working at Fizix Solar Innovation Pvt. Ltd., focused on solar and battery-management systems. My work spans protection circuits, voltage/current sensing, MOSFET and relay switching, KiCad-based PCB design, embedded firmware, hardware bring-up, and system debugging.

I have experience with ARM microcontrollers, ESP32, RTOS, and communication protocols including UART, I²C, SPI, LoRa, GSM, and GPS. I enjoy taking embedded systems from circuit design and firmware development through validation and real-world testing.
